We Do Great Things From Small Places.

🧠

Critical Thinking

Encourage concentration, planning, decision-making, and creative problem-solving through chess.

🎓

Education & Growth

Create opportunities for students to develop useful skills that support learning inside and outside the classroom.

🤝

Social Development

Promote teamwork, respect, confidence, sportsmanship, and meaningful connections among young people.
